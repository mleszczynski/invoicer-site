Invoicer

A small, self-hosted invoicing app that runs on your own computer.

Invoicer is a personal tool for freelancers: keep your company and customer details, create and duplicate monthly invoices, review the PDF, email it, and record payments. It watches a local folder for expense receipts and can add them to the invoice.

There is no hosted service and no account. Everything, including the database, receipts and invoice PDFs, stays on the computer where Invoicer is installed.

Google Drive backup

Optionally, Invoicer can copy your receipts and invoice PDFs to your own Google Drive. It asks only for the drive.file permission, so it can see and manage only the files it uploads itself, never the rest of your Drive. You can disconnect at any time.

Privacy

Invoicer does not collect, sell or share any data. Read the privacy policy.
